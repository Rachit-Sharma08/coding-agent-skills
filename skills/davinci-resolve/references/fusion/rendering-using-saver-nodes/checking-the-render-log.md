### Checking the Render Log


Inside the Render Manager dialog, the log file presents messages that help you work out why a render or node failed. That render log lays out a sequential record of what took place (or failed to take place) over the course of a render. Whenever a Render node can’t be located, fails to load a composition or to render a frame, or just quits responding, the event lands here.
