### Ease In/Out


When you want finer command over how long the Bézier direction handles on chosen keyframes are, reach for the Spline Ease dialog. Bring it up by clicking a keyframe in the graph and then either picking Edit > Ease In/Out off the graph’s contextual menu or tapping T on your keyboard. Up above the graph area, the Ease In/Out controls show themselves. Sizing the direction handles is then a matter of scrubbing across the number fields or typing a figure straight into them.
