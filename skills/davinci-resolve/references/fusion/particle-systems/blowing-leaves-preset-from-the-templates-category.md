### Example Particle Systems

Within the Templates category—reachable from the Inspector on the Fusion page of DaVinci Resolve, or from the Bins window in Fusion Studio—sit more than 20 distinct particle-system examples producing all kinds of effects. A great route to mastering how particle systems are built and customized is to open these and study their construction. An assortment of particle system presets sitting in the Templates category of Fusion Studio's Bins window Just drag any particle preset onto the Node Editor, load its final node into the viewer, and the assembly reveals itself.
