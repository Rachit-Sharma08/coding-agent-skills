### The Spreadsheet Editor


A distinct panel known as the Spreadsheet Editor can be shown directly under the Keyframes Editor. Its purpose is to lay out, in a tidy grid of rows and columns listing time against value, the exact numbers behind the keyframes for whichever parameters you have chosen in the Keyframes Editor’s header. You bring it up by clicking the Spreadsheet button on the toolbar, at which point it divides the Work Area panel and is shown just beneath the Keyframes Editor’s interface.
