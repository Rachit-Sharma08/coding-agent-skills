### Example: Assembling Clips Into the Timeline From the Media Pool


There is also the option to cut clips straight into the Timeline out of the Media Pool through several different commands. This often serves as a quick route for tacking clips onto the tail of the Timeline (though insert edits can be carried out the same way). To bring one or more Media Pool clips into the Timeline: 1 If needed, mark In and Out points on each clip you intend to place into the Timeline, relying for this on any of these: the Media pool thumbnails while in Thumbnail view, or the Media Pool Filmstrip Viewer while in List view, or simply loading each clip one at a time into the Source Viewer. With any of these methods, hit I to mark an In point and O to mark an Out point. 2 Adjust the sort order within the Media Pool’s browser region so the clips line up in the sequence you’d like them to appear. The Sort Order menu handles this in Thumbnail view, whereas in List view clicking the header of any metadata column sorts by the data in that column. 3 Park the playhead at the spot where the clips should land. 4 You can click, you can drag, you can use the Command-Option together with the Command-Shift Up and Down Arrow Key shortcuts, or instead reach for the Option-1–8 and Command-Option-1–8 key shortcuts to route the video and audio destination controls of the Source Track Selector onto the tracks meant to receive the incoming clip(s)’ video and audio. Clicking any destination control directly switches it off if you’d rather bring clips into the Timeline as audio-only or video-only. 5 Highlight a single clip or several. The following edit types each cope with multiple clips at once: insert, overwrite, place on top, ripple overwrite, and append at end. By contrast, the replace and fit to fill edits work on just one clip per pass and will bring only the first among several highlighted clips into the Timeline. 6 To carry out the edit, do one of the following:

- Drag the chosen clips onto the Timeline Viewer and release them over an editing overlay to trigger that particular edit type.
- Right-click one or more clips you’ve selected in the Media Pool and pick “Insert Selected Clips to Timeline,” or “Append Selected Clips to Timeline.”

That places the chosen clip(s) into the Timeline.
