### The Storage tab, showing the storage graph

To Buy More Storage: 1 On the Storage tab, choose Buy Storage. 2 Move the Storage Size slider to grant your organization additional storage space. 3 Press Add to Cart, then work through the checkout procedure to seal the purchase. To Remove Storage: 1 On the Storage tab, choose Remove Storage. 2 Drag the Storage Size slider so idle storage space is trimmed from your organization. 3 Press Continue. 4 A warning dialog tells you the surplus storage comes off right away and that the new plan’s total will be billed beginning on your next billing date. 5 Press Confirm Changes.

NOTE: Blackmagic Cloud Storage will never delete your media; that authority rests with you alone. Attempt to drop your storage beneath what you’re presently using and the downgrade is canceled on its own. You’ll need to manually clear out enough media to fit inside the new, smaller storage figure, then attempt the downgrade once more. What Happens to My Media if I Don’t Pay? Your media is never deleted by Blackmagic Cloud Storage. Should you stop paying the monthly fee and walk away from the account, then after 15 days past due and three emails your account is disabled rather than deleted. You lose access to that media but can revive the account by phoning customer service to switch it back on.

### Presentations

The Presentations tab shows how many presentations are in use and lets you buy more or scale back the number your Organization holds. The presentations currently in use appear in the layout, complete with their status and the count of clips each contains.

The Presentations tab, which displays the count and state of your organization’s presentations To Buy More Presentations: 1 On the Presentations tab, choose Buy Presentations. 2 Set the amount to grant your organization extra presentations. 3 Press Add to Cart, then work through the checkout procedure to seal the purchase. To Remove Presentations: 1 On the Presentations tab, choose Remove Presentations. 2 Move the Storage Size slider to cut unused Presentations from your organization. 3 Press Continue. Your Presentations can’t be dropped beneath the count presently in use. To delete still more presentations from your organization, you’ll first have to disable them over in the main Presentations section.

### Billing

Your organization’s billing details are governed from this tab. Here you can amend and refresh them, and pull down past invoices as well.

### The Billing tab

You’re able to amend and edit your organization’s billing details, enter a tax ID, and refresh or swap out your credit card info. Past invoices can also be downloaded here for accounting and tax purposes.

### Settings

From the Settings tab you choose who holds owner access to the organization, and you can delete the organization itself. The Profile section is where the organization name and logo get changed.

### The Settings tab

Organization Owners act as account administrators: they can bring in and take out members and groups, purchase storage, presentations, and licenses, and alter the cloud storage plan. Be sure they are people your organization places its trust in.

To Manage Organization Owners: 1 On the Settings tab, click Manage Owners. 2 To Add an Owner, key the person’s email or user name into the box provided. 3 To Remove an Owner, click the X icon next to that person’s name and then, in the warning dialog, click Remove. 4 Press Save. To Delete an Organization The deletion can be kicked off by any owner who clicks Delete Organization. The process runs for five days, and an email goes out to every current owner of the organization. During that window any owner can halt and reverse the deletion. Deleting an Organization is permanent and cannot be recovered.
