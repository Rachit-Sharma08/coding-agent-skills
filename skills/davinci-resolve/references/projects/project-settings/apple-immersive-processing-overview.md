### Apple Immersive Processing

This part lets you supply custom ILPD (Immersive Lens Profile Data) that alters how the immersive content gets calibrated and projected.

- Apple immersive calibration: Displays the name belonging to the calibration file. Opening the option menu lets you pick from a roster of calibration files you’ve used before.
- Projection format: Pick the projection format that the media files should use.
- Update Lists: Hit this button to reload the ILPD content reachable within the Media Pool.
- Open Calibration Folder: Hit this button to open the calibration folder on your local system, to

choose an ILPD file.
