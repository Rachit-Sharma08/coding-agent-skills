## Mark

### Mark

Mark In – Page 751 Places the In Point onto a clip or timeline. Mark Out – Page 751 Places the Out Point onto a clip or timeline. Mark Video In – Page 752 Builds an in point on a split edit's video track. Mark Video Out – Page 752 Builds an out point on a split edit's video track. Mark Audio In – Page 752 Builds an in point on a split edit's audio tracks. Mark Audio Out – Page 752 Builds an out point on a split edit's audio tracks. Convert In and Out to Duration Marker – Page 733 Converts the in and out points over to a duration marker. Set In and Out from Duration Marker – Page 753 Converts a duration marker back into In and Out Points. Clear In – Page 752 Strips the In Point off a clip or timeline. Clear Out – Page 752 Strips the Out Point off a clip or timeline. Clear In and Out – Page 752 Strips both the In and Out Points off a clip or timeline. Clear Video In and Out – Page 752 Wipes the In and Out Points from the Video track of a split edit. Clear Audio In and Out – Page 602 Wipes the In and Out Points from the Audio tracks of a split edit. Mark Clip – Page 840 Places In and Out Points for you according to a clip's duration. Mark Selection – Page 840 Places In and Out Points for you according to several clips' combined duration. Create SubClip – Page 465 Builds a subclip from a clip's In and Out points.

Keyframe Timeline Mode – Page 3314 Pick which keyframe types get copied across Color grades. Add Keyframe – Page 3282 Drops a Dynamic Keyframe where the playhead sits. Add Static Keyframe – Page 3413 Drops a Static Keyframe where the playhead sits. Delete Keyframe Strips a keyframe from where the playhead sits. Delete All Keyframes Strips every keyframe off a chosen parameter. Move Selected Keyframes Left Shifts all chosen keyframes 1 frame to the left. Move Selected Keyframes Right Shifts all chosen keyframes 1 frame to the right. Move Selected Keyframes Up Shifts all chosen keyframes upward by 1 unit. Move Selected Keyframes Down Shifts all chosen keyframes downward by 1 unit. Add Marker – Page 884 Drops a marker onto a clip or timeline by color. Add and Modify Marker – Page 1011 Drops a marker onto a clip or timeline and brings up the Marker properties dialog. Modify Marker – Page 887 Brings up the Marker properties dialog for the chosen marker. Delete Marker – Page 889 Removes the chosen Marker Delete All Markers – Page 889 Removes every marker on a clip or timeline, or those of a given marker color. Favorite Keywords – Page 355 Drops a favorite keyword from your list onto a clip. Add Flag – Page 883 Drops a flag onto a clip by color. Clear Flags – Page 733 Strips every flag off a chosen clip. Delete All Flags – Page 883 Strips every flag off all clips by color. Set Clip Color – Page 898 Alters a clip’s color within the timeline.
