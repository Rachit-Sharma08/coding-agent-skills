### Dynamic Zoom


The Dynamic Zoom controls, switched off to begin with, give you a quick, simple way to build pan and scan moves that zoom into or out of a clip. On top of that, when you bring in a project from Final Cut Pro X holding clips that lean on the Ken Burns effect, those clips' effects feed the Dynamic Zoom parameters in DaVinci Resolve. Switching the Dynamic Zoom group on lights up two Inspector controls that operate in tandem with the Dynamic Zoom onscreen adjustment controls you can surface in the Timeline Viewer (covered below):

- Dynamic Zoom Ease: Lets you set how the motion these controls create speeds up. The choices are Linear, Ease In, Ease Out, and Ease In and Out.
- Swap: This button flips the start and end transforms that build the dynamic zoom effect.
