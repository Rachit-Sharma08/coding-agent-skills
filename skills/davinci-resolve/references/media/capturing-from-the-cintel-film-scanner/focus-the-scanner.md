### Focus the Scanner


The same way a camera operator brings the lens into sharpness, you must bring the projected frame into sharpness against the sensor of your scanner. The route to dead-on focus is to enable the Focus Assist checkbox found in the Film Scanner capture settings within DaVinci Resolve. Doing so lays a focus peaking overlay across the Ultra HD picture coming out of the scanner's HDMI output, and that same overlay shows inside DaVinci Resolve's capture window. To get the cleanest read, hook an Ultra HD display up to your Cintel scanner, which lets you watch at the highest resolution on offer as you dial in focus. When Focus Assist is active, the peaking will latch onto the film grain of the scanned frame at the exact moment the film plane sits in true focus. That behavior makes it possible to focus the scanner itself even when the photographed image is soft. Just keep an eye on the scanner's Ultra HD output as you rotate the Cintel scanner's focus wheel. The picture has reached focus once the grain spread across the whole frame lights up with peaking outlines. To double-check the focus you've dialed in, look at the borders of your film's perforations — when those read crisp, the film is focused.
