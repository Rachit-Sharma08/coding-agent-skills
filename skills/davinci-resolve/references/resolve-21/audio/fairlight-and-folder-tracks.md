### Fairlight

### Folder Tracks

Arriving in Fairlight with DaVinci Resolve 21, Folder Tracks let you pick out a set of tracks and merge them into one composite view. Nothing happens to the underlying tracks themselves, yet the combined display now occupies just a single track's footprint along the timeline. That makes it simple to rescale and reframe how busy your timeline reads as the project grows more involved. Say you have four distinct gunfire sound-effect tracks backing a running action sequence and you'd rather roll them into one folder track, freeing you to mix the score without a cluttered UI full of tracks you don't care about. Inside the folder track, every individual track position still appears, only now indented beneath the track folder. Click the disclosure triangle at the folder track's upper left to collapse the whole thing down into one track. A second click on that disclosure triangle expands the folder track once more. While collapsed, the original clip positions render in a shrunken form with no waveform shown. Whatever track colors you set remain on display, and clicking the track name lets you rename the folder track. A closed folder track carries no level meters, though reopening it brings them straight back. You can edit directly on the folder track's composite view, which proves both handy and capable once many elements are in play. Solo and Mute buttons exist at the folder level too, handy when you'd like to solo or mute a whole batch of tracks together. Deleting the folder track is all it takes to bring the original tracks permanently back into view. TIP: A folder track can also live inside another folder track for finer-grained track organization. Folder tracks allow up to three levels of nesting at most.

### The folder track open, showing the enclosed tracks

The folder track collapsed by way of the disclosure triangle positioned beside the track header on its left. Steps for grouping into a folder: 1 On the Fairlight Page, highlight at least two tracks 2 Right-click whichever of the highlighted tracks you like 3 Choose Add Tracks to New Folder. Steps for removing the folder and returning each track to where it began: 1 On the folder track, open the right-click menu. 2 From the context menu that appears, pick Delete Track.

### Go to Mouse Pointer Using the C Key

As on the Edit Page, a tap of the “C” key jumps the playhead immediately to the pointer's location inside the Fairlight timeline. Snapping like this on the spot makes getting around lengthy timelines quicker and smoother.
