### Histogram Display Options

These options are offered by the Histogram:

- Either RGB or YRGB histograms can be displayed.
- Gain slider stretches that scope’s graph taller or flatter. Taller graphs grow to surface more detail

within the histogram’s peaks, while shorter graphs lessen how much detail is apparent.

- Graticule slider raises or lowers the brightness of that scope’s scale, rendering it more or less prominent

(or distracting) against the graph.

- The Show Reference Levels checkbox switches on adjustable Low and High reference

level markers once you move the Low and High sliders off their default positions. Such reference markers prove particularly handy in HDR grading, where you operate inside a defined peak luminance threshold.

### CIE Chromaticity Display Options

These options are offered by the CIE Chromaticity scope:

- A chromaticity analysis can be viewed either as a CIE 1931 xy graph or as a CIE 1976 uv graph.
- CIE Chromaticity slider raises or lowers the brightness of that scope’s graph. Brighter graphs reveal fine detail more readily, yet make it tougher to judge which portions of the graph are stronger or weaker.
- Graticule slider raises or lowers the brightness of that scope’s scale, rendering it more or less prominent (or distracting) against the graph.
- Additional Gamut drop-down menu surfaces a second gamut triangle, for those times when you want to weigh how an image sits within two distinct gamut ranges.
- Show 2X Zoom magnifies the CIE graph and graticule to 200%, which helps in reading fine detail and in pairing the vectorscope with the charts.
- Show Graticule checkbox toggles the visibility of both the circular degree markings around the outer rim and the cross hairs marking the vectorscope’s center.

### Resetting Scope Display Options

To return every scope to its default settings, click the scopes option menu and pick Reset All.
