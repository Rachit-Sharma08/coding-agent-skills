### Vocal Channel


This is a refinement plugin built for everyday vocal work, bundling High Pass filtering together with EQ and Compressor controls. Above those controls sit paired EQ and Dynamics graphs shown side by side. A dedicated output audio meter gives you a way to watch the finished signal that the plugin sends out.
