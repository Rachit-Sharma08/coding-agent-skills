### Quick Export

After your program is complete and you’re ready to pass it along to others, the Quick Export button can render the Timeline’s contents into a self‑contained file in any of several formats so you can hand it off to people.

From any page of DaVinci Resolve, picking File > Quick Export gives you access to one of several export presets for outputting your program. Quick Export will even render and upload your program straight to one of the supported video sharing services, such as YouTube, Vimeo, and Dropbox. To use Quick Export: 1 (Optional) From the Cut, Edit, Fusion, or Color page, place In and Out points on the Timeline to mark the portion of the current program you want exported. When no timeline In or Out points are present, the whole timeline gets exported. 2 Choose File > Quick Export. 3 From the top row of icons in the Quick Export dialog, pick the preset you want and press Export. 4 Through the Export dialog, pick a directory location, type a file name, and press Save. A progress bar dialog shows up so you can gauge how long the export will run.

### The Quick Export dialog showing a custom preset

To build a brand-new Custom Preset and place it into Quick Export: 1 Over on the Deliver page, pick Custom Export within the Render Settings and tune the Video, Audio, and File settings for the preset you’re building. Custom Presets accept only Single Clip settings, never Individual Clips. 2 From the Render Settings 3-Dot Option Menu, pick Save as New Preset. 3 Enter a name for the new Preset and select a preset icon to stand in for it. 4 Tick the Add to quick export box. 5 Press Save.

Your preset together with its icon now shows up in the Quick Export dialog throughout every page of DaVinci Resolve and is likewise added to the Render Settings over on the Deliver page. Shown circled within the Quick Export dialog is the List View icon. To switch the Quick Export Options over to List View: 1 Bring up the Quick Export dialog. 2 Click the List View icon, found within the Render Settings.

### Assign Icons to User Presets

Whenever you build a new Custom Preset on the Deliver page, beyond supplying a name you can now pick an icon from the list to represent it across the Render Settings and Quick Export dialog.
